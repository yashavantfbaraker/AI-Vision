AI Vision

See the world through your phone’s AI voice

AI Vision uses your camera and microphone to describe what is around you, read signs, boards and labels aloud, and answer your questions. Made for blind and low-vision people.

Features

Describe SurroundingsPeople, objects, doors, stairs and vehicles, with direction and distance in steps.
Read TextBoards, signs, menus, medicine strips, currency notes and bus numbers read aloud.
Ask a QuestionAsk “What colour is this shirt?” or “Is the road clear?” and hear the answer.
Safety AlertsHazards are spoken first, with a warning tone and strong vibration.
Auto-DescribeHands-free updates every 8 seconds while you walk.
6 LanguagesEnglish, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam.

How it works

1. OpenTap the big button. The camera starts and a voice greets you.
2. PointHold the phone steady in front of you or at the text.
3. Hold or tapHold anywhere on the screen, or press Describe Now. The AI speaks the answer.

Gestures

Hold 0.6 s: describe, read or listen. Single tap: repeat. Double tap: switch mode. Hold 2 s: emergency help.

Safety and privacy

The AI can make mistakes. It does not replace a cane, a guide dog or help from a person. Pictures and audio are used only to answer you and are not stored.

Questions

Does it work with TalkBack or VoiceOver?Yes. Every button is labelled, and a large Hold to Talk button is provided.
Why does it ask for the camera?The camera lets the AI see what is in front of you. If you refuse, use the Take a photo button.
Can I use my voice only?Yes. Hold the screen and say describe, read or ask.

What do you need?

Hold anywhere on this screen and say “describe”, “read” or “ask”.

Ready
Hold anywhere on the screen to start.

Settings

Help and Gestures

Hold 0.6 seconds anywhere: in Describe or Read mode, takes a picture and speaks. In Ask mode, listens while you hold, then answers when you let go.

Single tap: repeat the last answer.

Double tap: switch between Describe, Read and Ask.

Hold 2 seconds: emergency help message.

While the AI is speaking: hold to stop it.

The AI can make mistakes. It does not replace a cane, a guide dog or help from a person.
